Yu Li

I am a Ph.D. student at Institute of Artificial Intelligence, Peking University (PKU), advised by Prof. Yaodong Yang. I have been privileged to work closely with Prof. Hao Dong, Dr. Ruihai Wu, and Yuanpei Chen.

My research interests lie in robot self-improvement. Specifically, I am currently focusing on robot post-training via diffusion RL, real-world RL, and asynchronous robot deployment.
